# Saachi Profile Shot List

Goal: make Saachi's profile feel like a rigorous, sparkly, useful editorial feature. The visuals should prove the story: what she uses, what she uses it for, when it entered the stack, what survived hype, and what the tools reveal about her.

Everything should be real: real desk, real screenshots, real tools, real dashboards, real notes. Redact private details, but keep enough texture that it feels lived-in.

## Visual System

The page should have three levels of evidence:

1. **Numbered photo carousel**: one gorgeous tidy desk setup photo plus a long run of numbered detail images.
2. **Zoom/detail photos**: close-up photos/crops keyed to the hero numbers.
3. **In-text screenshots**: receipts inside the article showing actual systems, tools, dashboards, prompts, and outputs.

Structural reference: Into The Gloss Top Shelf pages. Lead with many numbered images and "zoom" energy, then move into categorized interview/article sections.

## Photo Direction

Aim for: editorial command center.

Not sterile. Not messy for the sake of being messy. Tidy enough to photograph, personal enough to read.

Keep visible:

- monitor/laptop setup
- wallpaper
- wall notes / inspo objects
- pens, notebook, cup, cards, small rituals
- outdoor/adventure clues
- one or two intentional windows on screens

Hide or redact:

- passwords
- private email addresses
- private phone numbers
- salary details
- unemployment/legal details
- sensitive company names if needed
- anything involving another person that would feel weird public
