Tender command center.

## Why This Profile Works

This is the right pilot because the visual world is already doing the concept. The desk has three layers:

- the physical setup: monitors, laptop, lamp, notes, cup, pens, papers, camera, cards
- the visual backdrop: mountain wallpaper, illustrated posters, training/adventure checklists, handwritten boards
- the digital surface: Outlook, LinkedIn, OneNote, browser tabs, documents, tools, dock icons

The format does not need to invent a personality. It just needs to annotate what is already there.

## Angle
