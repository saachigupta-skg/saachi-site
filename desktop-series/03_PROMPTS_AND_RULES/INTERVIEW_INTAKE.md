# Interview Intake

Send this to a subject:

```text
I'm making a portrait of your desktop as a setup tour / digital shelfie.

Can you send any of the following?

- a photo of you at your desk
- a screenshot of your desktop
- a screenshot of your dock/app bar
- the AI tools you actually use weekly
- what you pay for
- the thing that's always open
- one prompt you reuse
- one tool you quit
- one tool that is hype
- one tool that actually survived the novelty phase
- one weird AI use case
- one thing you refuse to outsource

Useful follow-up questions:

- What do you actually use weekly?
- What did you try and abandon?
- What is overhyped but still useful?
- What looked promising but did not survive real work?
- What do you pay for because it makes you feel more capable?
- What only works because of your specific job, taste, or context?

Messy is better than polished.
```
